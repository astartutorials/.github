## What we do

A-Star Tutorials runs group and private tutorials for university students, starting at
Babcock University. Our tutors are high-performing students who excelled in the courses
they teach, so they know what lecturers expect and where marks are lost.

- **Group and private tutorials**, online or in person, booked one session at a time
- **The Full Semester Plan**: 50 sessions from the first lecture to the last exam, paid once
- **Programmes and events** for freshers, like The Freshers' Advantage

## What we build

This organisation holds the platform behind [astartutorials.com](https://astartutorials.com):
booking and payments, tutor and ambassador applications, and the admin tools our team runs on.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Paystack and Vercel.
